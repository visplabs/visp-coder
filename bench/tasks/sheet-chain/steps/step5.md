Big sheets are a problem in the spreadsheet engine in this repository. Fill the whole grid with one long chain, where `A1` is a number, `A2` is `=A1+1`, and so on down each column and on to the top of the next one (`B1` is `=A99+1`) through `Z99`, then read every cell: today this takes far too long and then crashes.

Reading every cell of that sheet after building it should take a couple of seconds at most, with every value right. Editing it should feel instant too: a change should only cost work for the cells that depend on it, so setting `Z99` and reading `Z98` a few thousand times in a row, or changing `A1` and reading `Z99` a few dozen times, takes a couple of seconds at most. Everything else should work as it does now.
